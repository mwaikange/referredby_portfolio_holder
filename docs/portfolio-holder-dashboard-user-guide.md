# ReferredBy Portfolio Holder Dashboard

## User Guide

**For:** Portfolio holders, financiers and authorised bank users  
**Document status:** Draft v0.1  
**Prepared:** 24 July 2026

## 1. Welcome

The ReferredBy Portfolio Holder Dashboard gives you a secure, read-only view of your funded loan portfolio.

You can use the dashboard to:

- See how much capital has been allocated and deployed
- Review available capital and the current portfolio value
- Monitor loans and collections
- View the lending societies funded by your portfolio
- Identify overdue amounts and portfolio risk
- Download information for your own reporting

The dashboard is designed for monitoring. It does not allow you to approve loans, change records, make payments or edit ReferredBy operational data.

## 2. Signing in

1. Open the Portfolio Holder Portal.
2. Enter the email address registered for your portfolio.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Sign in**.

After signing in, the dashboard only displays information linked to your portfolio-holder account.

### If you have forgotten your password

Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

### Keeping your account secure

- Do not share your password.
- Sign out when using a shared computer.
- Contact ReferredBy support if you notice information that does not belong to your portfolio.
- Contact support immediately if you believe somebody else has accessed your account.

## 3. Dashboard navigation

The menu on the left-hand side gives you access to the main areas of the portal.

| Menu item | What it is used for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | A high-level summary of the portfolio |
| **Portfolios** | A detailed view of capital position and performance |
| **Lending societies** | Organisations funded by the portfolio |
| **Disbursements** | Loans awaiting approval or disbursement |
| **Loans** | The complete loan book available to your account |
| **Collections** | Payments received and collection performance |
| **Risk & ratings** | Arrears, portfolio score and risk indicators |
| **Reports & exports** | Downloadable portfolio information |
| **Audit log** | Reserved for approved portfolio activity records |
| **Settings** | Account and portal options |

On smaller screens, some information may be arranged vertically. Use the faint scrollbar on the right when a page contains more information than can fit on the screen.

## 4. Overview

![Portfolio overview](C:/Users/clien/AppData/Local/Temp/codex-clipboard-c624e96d-3a9f-48fe-9ee6-236f28ed0648.png)

The Overview page is the quickest way to understand the current position of your portfolio.

### Main figures

#### Portfolio value

The estimated current value of the portfolio, combining available capital and money still outstanding on active loans.

#### Cash on hand

The estimated capital currently available after allowing for money deployed to loans and collections received.

> Cash on hand is a dashboard estimate. It should not be treated as a bank-account balance unless it has been formally reconciled.

#### Capital deployed

The total value that has been placed into qualifying loans, including loans that may since have been settled.

#### Collections

The total loan payments recorded for the portfolio.

#### Active exposure

The value still outstanding on active loans.

#### Book performance

The difference between the estimated current portfolio value and the original capital allocation.

- A positive amount means the estimated portfolio value is above the original allocation.
- A negative amount means it is below the original allocation.

### Capital movement

This chart compares:

- **Disbursements:** capital placed into loans
- **Collections:** money received from borrowers

Use it to identify months in which lending or collections increased or decreased.

### Performance score

The performance score provides a combined view of collection performance, arrears, capital use, return, available capital and settled loans.

It is a monitoring indicator, not a credit guarantee or audited investment rating.

### Capital composition

This section shows how the portfolio is currently divided between:

- Capital estimated to be available
- Capital still exposed through active loans

### Lending society snapshot

This section highlights the societies receiving the most funding from the portfolio. Select **View all** to open the complete Lending societies page.

### Exporting the overview

Select **Export snapshot** to download the current summary as a CSV file. CSV files can be opened in Microsoft Excel or another spreadsheet application.

## 5. Portfolios

![Portfolio detail](C:/Users/clien/AppData/Local/Temp/codex-clipboard-7da8cf58-7dfb-4650-824d-edf5240bbfb1.png)

The Portfolios page brings the main financial position and performance ratios together in one place.

### Portfolio summary

The summary includes:

- Initial allocation
- Current portfolio value
- Book return
- Loans more than 30 days in arrears

### Capital position

This section shows how the original allocation has moved:

- **Initial allocation:** capital assigned to the portfolio
- **Capital deployed:** capital placed into loans
- **Collections received:** payments recorded
- **Cash on hand:** estimated available capital

### Performance ratios

#### Utilisation

Shows how much of the allocated capital has been deployed.

A low percentage may mean that much of the capital has not yet been used. A high percentage means more of the allocation has been deployed into loans.

#### Collection efficiency

Compares payments received with payments that were due.

A higher percentage generally indicates stronger collection performance.

#### Cash ratio

Shows the share of the portfolio value estimated to be available as cash.

#### Book return

Shows the estimated performance of the portfolio compared with the original allocation.

### Portfolio tabs

- **Summary** displays the current portfolio position.
- **Loan book** opens the Loans page.
- **Collections** opens the Collections page.
- **Risk & arrears** opens Risk & ratings.
- **Documents** is reserved for a future document service.

Select **Export CSV** to download the portfolio summary.

The Disbursements page shows loans that are still in the approval or pre-disbursement process.

You may see:

- Loan reference
- Masked borrower reference
- Lending society
- Loan type
- Loan amount
- Relevant date
- Current status

### Common statuses

| Status | Plain-language meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting approval** | The loan is still being reviewed |
| **Awaiting disbursement** | The loan is approved or prepared for payment but not yet reflected as an active disbursed loan |

The page is for monitoring only. Portfolio holders cannot approve, reject, pay or batch loans from this portal.

## 8. Loans

The Loans page shows the loan records available to your portfolio.

Typical information includes:

- Loan reference
- Masked borrower identifier
- Lending society
- Nano or term loan type
- Original loan amount
- Outstanding balance
- Relevant dates
- Current status

### Searching and filtering

Use the search field to find a loan by the information displayed on the page. Use the status filter to limit the list to loans in a particular stage.

Select **Export** to download the current loan information.

### Loan statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not requested** | The loan has not entered the approval process |
| **Awaiting approval** | The loan is being reviewed |
| **Awaiting disbursement** | The loan is waiting to be paid or activated |
| **Due** | Payment is currently due |
| **Outstanding** | The loan still has an unpaid balance |
| **Blocked** | The loan is restricted and requires operational attention |
| **Paid up** | The loan has been settled |
| **Declined** | The loan application was not approved |

If a status appears inconsistent with the loan information, contact ReferredBy support rather than relying on your own interpretation.

## 9. Collections

The Collections page shows payments recorded against the portfolio’s loans.

### Summary figures

#### Amount due

The total scheduled payment amount shown for the selected portfolio data.

#### Amount received

The total amount recorded as paid.

#### Collection efficiency

A comparison of payments received with payments due.

### Monthly collections

The chart compares scheduled and received amounts by month. Use it to spot changes in repayment performance.

### Recent payments

The recent-payment list may include:

- Payment reference
- Loan reference
- Lending society
- Amount due
- Amount received
- Payment date
- Days late

Select **Export** to download collection information as CSV.

### Important note

Recently submitted, reversed or failed payments may require operational reconciliation before they can be treated as cleared funds.

## 10. Risk & ratings

![Risk and ratings review image](C:/Users/clien/AppData/Local/Temp/codex-clipboard-66b949e3-51dc-469d-a9f9-604bf5b54230.png)

> This image is from an earlier review version. The live dashboard identifies the score as a calculated monitoring indicator.

The Risk & ratings page helps you identify portfolio areas that may require attention.

### Portfolio score

The portfolio score combines several performance indicators into a score out of 100.

It considers:

- Collection efficiency
- Loans in arrears
- Capital utilisation
- Estimated portfolio return
- Available capital
- Settled loans

A higher score generally indicates stronger results under the current dashboard rules. The score must be read together with the underlying figures and should not be used alone for an investment or lending decision.

### Arrears ageing

Unpaid scheduled amounts are grouped by how late they are:

| Group | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Current** | Not overdue |
| **1–29 days** | Overdue by fewer than 30 days |
| **30+ days** | Overdue by 30 days or more |

### PAR 30+

PAR 30+ highlights the portion of active exposure associated with amounts at least 30 days overdue under the dashboard’s current reporting method.

A rising value may indicate increasing repayment risk and should be investigated.

### Society ratings

Where available, society ratings come from the latest rating information supplied to the portal. A missing rating means no approved rating is available in the current view.

## 11. Reports & exports

The Reports & exports page allows you to download available portfolio information.

Available exports may include:

- Portfolio summary
- Loan book
- Collections
- Lending society information
- Risk snapshot

### Working with exported files

1. Select the report you need.
2. Download the CSV file.
3. Open it in Excel or another spreadsheet application.
4. Confirm the report date and portfolio name before using the information.

Exports are working data extracts. They are not automatically audited financial statements or signed regulatory returns.

## 12. Audit log

The Audit log area is reserved for approved portfolio activity records.

If the page says that the audit service is not connected, this means no safe portfolio-holder audit view is currently available. The portal will not display made-up activity to fill this area.

## 13. Reading the dashboard responsibly

### Check the refresh date

Where shown, the **Data refreshed** date indicates when the portal last received the displayed information.

### Compare related figures

Do not judge performance from one number alone. For example:

- High utilisation can be positive, but should be considered with arrears and collections.
- High cash availability may provide flexibility, but may also mean capital has not been deployed.
- Strong collections should be considered with the amount actually due.
- A positive book return should be reviewed together with portfolio risk.

### Understand calculated values

Some dashboard values are calculated from operational records and may not equal:

- A bank-account balance
- An audited accounting balance
- A regulatory return
- A final settlement amount

Use the dashboard for portfolio monitoring and contact ReferredBy if you require a reconciled or formally certified figure.

## 14. Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

- Confirm that you are using the registered portfolio-holder email address.
- Check that Caps Lock is off.
- Use **Forgot password?** if necessary.
- Contact support if your account is not recognised.

### I cannot see my portfolio

Your login may not yet be linked to the correct portfolio-holder record. Contact ReferredBy support and provide your registered email address and organisation name.

### A figure appears incorrect

Record:

- The page name
- The figure concerned
- The date and time
- Any loan, payment or society reference shown

Send these details to ReferredBy support. Do not include your password.

### A page shows “Not available”

The source information may be missing, restricted or not yet approved for portfolio-holder access. It does not necessarily indicate an error.

### An export does not open correctly

Open the downloaded `.csv` file from within Excel and confirm that the currency and date columns are recognised correctly.

## 16. Support

Contact ReferredBy support for:

- Account access problems
- Incorrect portfolio assignment
- Questions about a loan or payment status
- Requests for reconciled figures
- Suspected security or privacy issues

When requesting support, include your organisation name and the relevant page or record reference. Never send your password.
